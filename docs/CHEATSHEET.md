# Tuna: quick cheat sheet

What every control does, by what you want to do. The [README](../README.md) has the whole story,
[SPEC.md](SPEC.md) all 89 composers and styles.

**Getting around:** KNOB 1 to 4 turn the four cards on the screen. A page's button opens it; HOME takes you back to
the falling notes.

## Play

| What | How |
| --- | --- |
| Start / stop | **PLAY**: always from the top (the same seed plays the same music) |
| Tempo / volume | **SELECT** / **MASTER** |
| Another piece | **PRESETS**: piece by piece; **ALGORITHM**: a whole era (or decade) at a time. Playing, it comes in at the next half of the period |
| The next seed | **OCT-** / **OCT+**: 256 a composer, sorted by the type of work ("FUGUE 17/86") |
| The root, a chord | **Keys**: tap one, the root from the next chord; hold several, that chord while held |
| Calm to busy | HOME, KNOB 4: **MOOD**, every part at once |
| Chaos | HOME, KNOB 1 to 3: **RHYTHM**, **PITCH**, **DENSITY** (0: the composer exactly) |
| Lock the phrase | **REC**: the phrase locked (deja vu) / the composer's own again |
| A favourite | **REC** held: the piece a favourite (held again: no longer); they wait below TUNA in the library |

## The library

| What | How |
| --- | --- |
| Browse | **FX**: LIBRARY. ERA turned below TUNA: the favourites |
| One manner | FX, KNOB 4: the manner (a fugue, a chorale, a nocturne...) |
| Share a piece | The share code under the list (`BACH-FUGUE-17`): the same piece on any FM-1, and as a link in the browser |
| Radio | Hold **HOME**: the MENU, RADIO and its minutes (1 to 30): another piece at a cadence |

## Shape it

| What | How |
| --- | --- |
| Key, scale, tuning | **SCL**: key, scale, tuning (equal, meantone, Werckmeister, Pythagorean, just, your own), memory |
| Time and parts | **EDIT**: time; again: the parts; again: which parts follow the chaos knobs |
| Evolution | **ARP**: the evolution timer (an energy arc that builds and modulates) |
| Freeze | **SEQ**: the period you hear into an editable pattern (up to 384 notes, trig conditions, 3 locks a step) |
| Mix and reverb | **GLO**: the mix; again: reverb (SEND, SIZE, DAMPING, TYPE: BATHROOM, ROOM, HALL, CATHEDRAL, SPRING) |
| Resonator | **LFO**: sympathetic strings on the mix, tuned to the chord, the key or the root |

## Keep and connect

| What | How |
| --- | --- |
| Save / load | **SAVE**: save; again: load. The 16 white keys are the slots; OCT- / OCT+: back / do it |
| MIDI out | **ENV**: every part its own channel (bass 1, melody 2, pad 3, timpani 4, drums 10), with General MIDI programs |
| MIDI clock | **ENV**: CLOCK off, out or in (in: Tuna follows the tempo, start and stop) |
| Colour, style | Hold **HOME**: the MENU |
