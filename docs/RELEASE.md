# Tuna 1.2

Generative music on the FM-1, composed as it plays.

New in 1.2, the player:

- Seeds sorted by the type of work: OCT+ walks through a composer's fugues, then chorales, then gigues, the screen
  saying where you are ("FUGUE 17/86").
- Favourites: hold REC on a piece you like; up to 32, kept across power-offs, below TUNA in the library.
- Share codes: every piece has one (BACH-FUGUE-17), the same piece on any FM-1 and in the browser.
- Radio: another piece after 1 to 30 minutes, from the same era or the whole library, coming in at a cadence.
- MOOD in place of MACRO: one knob from calm to busy, every part at once.
- MIDI clock in: Tuna follows another device's tempo, start and stop.
- Flute and sax as FM patches of Tuna's own, freeing the flash their samples took; WIND and RAIN on Felucca's
  NOISE engine.
- An instrument tester in the browser.

From 1.1: newer FM-1 units, with a UC25HQ80 flash, save (Felucca's fix for #320), and the sample clock plays for
days without a hiccup.

What Tuna holds:

- 89 composers and styles in 10 eras, from Hildegard von Bingen to today, in 285 manners; 86 popular genres in
  8 decades, the 1950s to now; 8 character presets, from STILL to DRIVE.
- The melodies of 45 composers tuned toward what 3,486 public-domain scores of theirs measure.
- 95 instruments on Felucca's engines, levels evened out; odd meters and grouped beats; equal, meantone,
  Werckmeister, Pythagorean, just and user-defined tunings.
- The chaos knobs RHYTHM, PITCH and DENSITY, and MOOD; a tapped key sets the root, held keys the chord; the seed on
  OCT− / OCT+; REC locks the phrase.
- A sequencer: freeze what you hear into an editable pattern of up to 384 notes, with trig conditions, parameter
  locks and an evolution timer.
- 16 slots that save the whole state and play back exactly the music that was saved.
- MIDI out with every part on its own channel, and clock; a resonator of sympathetic strings tuned to the harmony.
- In the browser: the same firmware on a simulated FM-1.

Runs on the FM-1. To go back: Felucca's installer, or the official V15.
